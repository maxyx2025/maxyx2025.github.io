VR Murder Mystery

Qingdu - VR Murder Mystery

Invite your friends, and pick a script. The moment the game begins, the whole party steps into that script's world — an immersive scene built for the story, from a storm-locked manor to a neon-lit city.

Through VR headsets, each player controls a full-body avatar and plays a character written into the script. Guided by the host, the group searches for clues, interrogates one another, and pieces together the truth — every role holding a different fragment of the mystery.
